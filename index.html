Orbital Drift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Orbital Drift, the Android live wallpaper app published by krino, with package name com.krinosrl.orbitaldrift. For privacy questions, contact krinosrl@gmail.com.

Personal data and tracking

Orbital Drift does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data through the app. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDKs or app-operated crash-reporting service. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifier. The app does not request Internet permission. Its wallpaper models and textures are bundled with the app and render on your device.

Settings and device information used locally

Your selected spacecraft, destination, destination cycle, schedule and motion preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Orbital Drift uses the device's current time and time zone to apply schedules, and screen visibility, power-saving state, memory classification and graphics capabilities to manage rendering. Wallpaper gestures are handled locally. This information is not sent to the developer or a third party by the app.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you change them, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. The app disables Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup of its settings. There is no developer-hosted user database or app account to delete.

Google Play, Android and external links

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, purchase or diagnostic information according to their own terms, privacy policies and your device settings. Store payments are handled by Google Play; Orbital Drift does not receive your payment card details. Google may make store reports or diagnostic reports available to the developer through Play Console. These platform services are separate from the app's local wallpaper operation. Google's privacy policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy . If you open an artwork credit or another external link, your browser connects to that website, whose privacy policy applies. Orbital Drift does not attach personal data to those links.

Contacting support

If you choose to email krinosrl@gmail.com, the developer receives the email address and information you include in your message. These are used to respond to your enquiry and provide support, retained only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations, and are not used for advertising. Email is handled through Gmail, subject to Google's privacy policy. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address.

Children's privacy

Orbital Drift does not knowingly collect personal data through the app from children or other users. If a child has provided personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request its deletion.

This privacy-policy website

The public policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer has added no advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, forms or cookies to this page. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and standard request information to operate and secure its hosting service, according to its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . This website processing is separate from the offline Android app.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its data practices. The latest revision date appears at the top of the policy. Developer: krino. Privacy contact: krinosrl@gmail.com.
